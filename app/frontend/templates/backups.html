Backup scope

Recovery backups include extracted document text and are uploaded to /{{ google_drive_folder_name }} in the Google Drive root as encrypted .json.gz.enc files. Original PDF, JPG and PNG files are not included.

Recovery backup risks and limits
  • If you lose the Recovery Key, encrypted backups cannot be restored.
  • Anyone with both the encrypted file and Recovery Key can read its document data; keep them separately and securely.
  • Backups preserve extracted text and document data, but not the original PDF, JPG, or PNG files.
  • Deleting an archive from Google Drive removes that recovery copy.
Restore recovery backup

Restores document text without original PDF, JPG or PNG files. Standard documents are indexed again automatically.
